<threat_model>
- **T2-01 over-capture:** audio continues after stop/cancel or max duration. Mitigate with one authoritative session owner, explicit frame/duration bounds, stop-before-close, and terminal-state tests.
- **T2-02 unbounded memory:** a fast callback grows a queue indefinitely. Mitigate with a fixed-capacity non-blocking handoff, typed overflow outcome, and a test asserting the maximum retained frame count.
- **T2-03 sensitive artifact leakage:** samples or machine paths enter events, JSON, or fixture metadata. Mitigate with immutable local artifacts, checksum-only diagnostics, redacted fixture manifests, and assertions that serialized events contain no sample/path content.
- **T2-04 teardown loss:** cancellation or backend failure leaves a live handle or hides a cleanup failure. Mitigate by reusing `AudioCapture.close()`, retaining failed handles for retry as Phase 1 does, and testing stop/close ordering.
</threat_model>
