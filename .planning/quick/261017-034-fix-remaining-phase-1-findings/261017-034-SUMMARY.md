## Behavior covered

- A close exception leaves the same stream handle attached, records a typed
  failure, leaves the capture active/failed, and does not emit
  `CAPTURE_CLOSED`.
- A later successful close clears the handle and emits `CAPTURE_CLOSED`.
- Open-validation and start cleanup failures also retain their handles for a
  retry instead of silently discarding them.
- Probe cleanup failure changes `audio.input` to `unsupported`, records the
  cleanup failure, and reports `closed=false`/`active=true`.
- `make test` has no host-Python fallback; it rejects missing environments and
  Python versions outside `>=3.12,<3.13`.
